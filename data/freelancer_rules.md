# Tax Rules for Freelancers (Self-Employed)

## Estimated Taxes
- **Requirement:** As a freelancer, you are both the employee and employer. You must pay self-employment tax (Social Security and Medicare) and income tax.
- **Payment Schedule:** Taxes are typically paid in four quarterly installments throughout the year. The deadlines are usually April 15, June 15, September 15, and January 15 of the next year.
- **Penalty:** If you pay too little tax through estimated payments, you may have to pay a penalty.

## Business Expenses
- **Definition:** You can deduct expenses that are both "ordinary" (common and accepted in your type of business) and "necessary" (helpful and appropriate for your business).
- **Common Examples:**
  - Office supplies (pens, paper, software subscriptions).
  - Business travel costs (airfare, hotels for work trips).
  - Marketing and advertising costs.
  - A portion of your internet and phone bills.

## Health Insurance Premiums
- **Deductibility:** Self-employed individuals may be able to deduct 100% of the health insurance premiums they pay for themselves, their spouse, and their dependents.
- **Condition:** This deduction is only available if you are not eligible to participate in a health plan sponsored by an employer (including your spouse's employer).
