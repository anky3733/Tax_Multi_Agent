# Tax Rules for Freelancers in Germany (Freiberufler)

## Tax Registration and Numbers

**Required Registrations:**
- **Tax Office Registration:** Register freelance activity with local Finanzamt
- **Tax ID (Steuer-ID):** Permanent 11-digit number for all tax purposes
- **Tax Number (Steuernummer):** Assigned by local tax office for business activities

**Professional Categories (Freie Berufe):**
- Legal professions (lawyers, notaries)
- Medical professions (doctors, therapists, pharmacists)
- Technical professions (architects, engineers, IT consultants)
- Creative professions (writers, artists, designers)
- Educational professions (teachers, trainers)

---

## Income Tax Obligations

**Tax Declaration Requirements:**
- **Annual Filing:** Mandatory submission by July 31 (or January 31 with tax advisor)
- **Electronic Filing:** Must use ELSTER portal for submissions
- **Income Reporting:** All freelance income must be declared using Anlage S

**Estimated Tax Payments (Vorauszahlungen):**
- **Quarterly Payments:** Based on previous year's tax liability
- **Payment Dates:** March 10, June 10, September 10, December 10
- **Adjustments:** Can request changes if income significantly differs from estimate

**Record Keeping:**
- **Invoice Records:** All client invoices with dates and amounts
- **Expense Documentation:** Receipts for all business expenses
- **Bank Statements:** Business account statements
- **Retention Period:** Minimum 10 years for tax-relevant documents

---

## Business Expense Deductions

**Fully Deductible Expenses:**
- Office supplies and materials
- Professional software and subscriptions
- Business insurance premiums
- Professional development and training
- Business travel and accommodation
- Marketing and advertising costs
- Professional memberships and subscriptions

**Partially Deductible Expenses:**
- Home office costs (if used exclusively for business)
- Internet and telephone (business portion only)
- Vehicle expenses (business use percentage)
- Meals during business travel (limited amounts)

**Non-Deductible Expenses:**
- Personal living expenses
- Private insurance premiums
- Personal vehicle use
- Entertainment and gifts (with exceptions)

---

## VAT Regulations (Umsatzsteuer)

**Standard VAT Obligations:**
- **Rate:** 19% standard rate (7% for some services)
- **Registration:** Automatic if not eligible for small business rule
- **Monthly/Quarterly Returns:** Depending on annual turnover
- **Input VAT:** Can reclaim VAT on business purchases

**Small Business Rule (Kleinunternehmerregelung):**
- **Eligibility:** Annual revenue under €22,000 (previous year) and under €50,000 (current year)
- **Benefits:** No VAT collection or payment required
- **Limitations:** Cannot reclaim input VAT on purchases
- **Election:** Can opt out of small business rule voluntarily

---

## Social Security Considerations

**Pension Insurance:**
- **Mandatory Professions:** Some freelance professions require pension contributions
- **Voluntary Participation:** Option to join statutory pension system
- **Private Alternatives:** Private pension plans and life insurance

**Health Insurance:**
- **Mandatory Coverage:** All residents must have health insurance
- **Options:** Statutory (gesetzliche) or private (private) health insurance
- **Premium Deductibility:** Basic coverage fully deductible as special expenses
