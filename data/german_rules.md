# Key Concepts of the German Tax System

## Tax Filing Portal (ELSTER)
- **Definition:** ELSTER (*Elektronische Steuererklärung*) is the official, secure online portal of the German tax authorities (*Finanzamt*).
- **Requirement:** Filing taxes electronically through ELSTER is mandatory for freelancers (*Freiberufler*) and most other taxpayers.

## Tax Numbers (Steuernummern)
- **Tax ID (Steueridentifikationsnummer):** A unique, permanent identification number issued to every resident at birth or upon registration. It is used for all tax purposes throughout your life.
- **Tax Number (Steuernummer):** A separate number issued by your local tax office (*Finanzamt*) once you register a business or freelance activity. It is used for your regular tax declarations and VAT returns.

## VAT for Small Businesses (Kleinunternehmerregelung)
- **Definition:** A special regulation for small businesses, including freelancers.
- **Eligibility:** If your annual revenue is below **22,000 €** in the first year and not expected to exceed **50,000 €** in the current year, you can opt to be a *Kleinunternehmer*.
- **Effect:** As a *Kleinunternehmer*, you do not charge VAT (*Umsatzsteuer*) on your invoices, which simplifies your accounting. However, you also cannot reclaim VAT on your own business purchases.
